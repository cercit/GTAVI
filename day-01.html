Day 1 of 9 · 11 November

the 13-year wait

GTA V came out in September 2013. GTA VI comes out on 19 November 2026. In most companies, the person who proposed that gap wouldn't survive the first review meeting.

13 yrsbetween GTA V and GTA VI
200M+copies of GTA V sold (Take-Two)
3console generations GTA V was sold on

01The decision

Every normal rule in product says ship faster, ship often, stay in front of the customer. In 13 years smartphones changed completely and two console generations came and went. Rockstar released one more main game in that time, Red Dead Redemption 2, and let GTA sit.

That looks like a gap. It wasn't one, because the old product never stopped earning.

02Why they could afford it

GTA V didn't end at launch. Rockstar released it on PS3 and Xbox 360, then again on PS4, Xbox One and PC, then again on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Same game, three generations of buyers. Each re-release found people who hadn't bought it before, or who bought it again.

Next to it sat GTA Online, which launched two weeks after the game and kept getting free updates for years. Players kept coming back, and some of them kept spending. More on that tomorrow.

The wait was never a gap with no income. The old product was paying for the new one the whole time.

03The PM lesson

What I take from it

Speed is a choice, not a rule. Shipping fast matters when your current product is dying and you need the next one. When your current product is still earning, you have something most teams never get, which is time to build the next thing properly.

The hard part is being honest about which situation you're in. Most of us assume we're in the first one.

04Where I'd push back

This only works if you have a GTA V. Most products don't sell for a decade, and a team that copies "take your time" without that cushion just runs out of money. The wait also wasn't calm. GTA VI itself was delayed twice, and Take-Two's share price moved both times. That's Day 5.
