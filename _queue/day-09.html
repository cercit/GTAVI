Thirteen years in the making

02

Who it's for

Rockstar hasn't published who it's building for. These are the people the launch is aimed at.

The story player

Has waited thirteen years for a new GTA story. On 19 November, that's all there is: Take-Two calls it "a single-player experience".

The new-console owner

PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar says it "plays best on PlayStation 5". Older Xboxes can only stream it through the cloud.

The collector

Pays $20 more for the Ultimate Edition: extra cars, weapons, clothes and missions woven into the story.

The GTA Online regular

Gets a free month of GTA+ with a digital pre-order. It works in GTA Online and the older games, not in GTA VI.

03

The problem it solves

For players

No new Grand Theft Auto story since 2013. GTA Online kept adding things for a decade, but it wasn't a new game.

Rockstar said in 2022 that players had been asking, and that the next game would "significantly move beyond" what it had made before.

For Take-Two

Grand Theft Auto products were 12.6% of Take-Two's revenue in the six months before launch, with no new game. Its own filing warns that "the timing of our Grand Theft Auto product releases may affect our financial performance".

GTA VI is the release its whole year is planned around.

Vice City

"Pre-orders for Grand Theft Auto VI are tracking far in excess of our initial expectations."

Strauss Zelnick, Take-Two's CEO, ten days before launch. He didn't give a number.
04

How it makes money

  • The gameDirect$79.99 for the standard edition in the US. Everything bought before 20 November also gets the Vintage Vice City Pack. Sold on the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store and in shops, where the box holds a download code, not a disc.
  • The Ultimate EditionDirect$99.99: $20 more for premium vehicles, weapons, clothes and extra action through the story. Rockstar hasn't said how many people chose it.
  • In-game purchasesLaterThe store lists in-game purchases, but no prices for them have been announced.
  • A month of GTA+IndirectA free month of GTA+ comes with digital pre-orders, for GTA Online, not GTA VI. It then renews at $7.99 a month unless you cancel.
05

The launch playbook

A launch is built long before launch day. Here's how Rockstar built this one, step by step.

  1. Confirm, then go quietFebruary 2022: "well underway". Then almost two years of nothing official.
  2. One trailer, a whole worldDecember 2023: Vice City, two names, a year. 93 million views in a day.
  3. Move the date, keep the promiseTwo delays, both explained the same way: quality. "Please tell me you're kidding," Zelnick said when the second was asked for, Bloomberg reported. Then he agreed.
  4. Price it lastThe price came in June 2026, five months before launch, with pre-orders and the free GTA+ month.
  5. Show the real gameAugust 2026: in-game footage, on Netflix first. October: a launch trailer and a famous composer.
  6. Make day one easyPre-loading nine days early, and a code in the box so physical buyers can pre-load too.

Zelnick told the Wall Street Journal that a "very high profile release" had shipped on schedule and "had to pull the title after shipping because it didn't work". He used it to explain why GTA VI could wait.

What people complain about

Complaint

Two delays

From a 2025 window to May 2026, then to November 2026. Rockstar apologised both times.

Complaint

30fps, even on PS5 Pro

Rockstar's target is 30fps on every console. IGN reported fans split over it, especially people who bought a PS5 Pro.

Complaint

$80 for a code in a box

A higher price than most new games, no disc in the box, and a download of up to 212 GB.

Complaint

No PC version

PC players have no date. GTA V reached PC in April 2015, about a year and a half after it launched on consoles.

08

What I'd change

Rockstar's launch is careful: it delayed for quality, priced late and made day one easy. The friction is in the things it hasn't said.

As a PM, I'd do three things. Publish a PC window, even a rough one, so PC players aren't left guessing. Say what's coming online and when, because GTA Online players are deciding now whether to keep paying for GTA+. And give the 30fps choice a clear reason, ideally with a performance option for PS5 Pro owners who paid for one.

PM concept · Learn this

Go-to-market strategy

Go-to-market is the plan for how a product reaches people: who it's for, what it costs, where it's sold, and the order in which people hear about it.

GTA VI's plan ran for almost three years: one trailer to set the world, delays explained as quality, a price five months out, real footage on Netflix, and pre-loading so day one works. The free GTA+ month even keeps GTA Online players paying while they wait.

The question for any PM: why reveal so little for so long? Because the audience was already there. A launch this big doesn't need to create demand. It needs to protect it until the game is ready.
