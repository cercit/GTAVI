01

What's behind the door

Rockstar calls GTA+ "the premium Membership for GTA Online players". Behind the door are five kinds of benefit.

GTA$

Cash every month

GTA$500,000 paid into your in-game bank on your billing day, plus member-only Shark Cards with 15% bonus GTA$.

100

The Vinewood Club

A 100-car garage over five floors, a workshop to modify cars, a showroom with test drives and member discounts, and a phone app.

NEW

Monthly drops

A bonus car to claim, liveries, Chameleon paint, clothes, and GTA$ and RP boosts. A new set arrives roughly every month.

CEO

Boss perks

CEO and VIP abilities without spending GTA$: request vehicles, bribe the police, go off the radar. Free taxi rides too.

10

Ten games

GTA V, GTA Online, Red Dead Redemption, Bully, L.A. Noire, the GTA Trilogy and more, for as long as each stays in the library.

03

The problem it solved

For players

GTA Online prices the big things in millions of GTA$. Until 2022 there were two ways to get them: grind, or buy Shark Cards.

GTA+ is a third way: a fixed monthly fee for a steady drip of cash, a car to claim, and time-savers like free taxis and a garage that holds a whole collection.

For Rockstar

A game first sold in 2013 now also earns every month, on top of Shark Card sales. A monthly charge is easier to plan around than one-off purchases.

It also gives Rockstar a reason to send players something new every month, which is the habit a live game needs.

05

How it makes money

  • The monthly chargeDirect$7.99 a month in the US, renewing until you cancel. There's only a monthly plan, no yearly price. On consoles it's sold on the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store, and inside GTA Online. On PC you can buy it from the Rockstar Store, Microsoft Store or Steam.
  • More Shark Card salesIndirectMembers get Shark Cards with 15% bonus GTA$. A Great White gives a member GTA$1,725,000 instead of GTA$1,500,000, for the same price. The membership makes the cards look like better value instead of replacing them.
  • Players who stayIndirectTake-Two reports GTA+ together with GTA Online's virtual currency, inside "recurrent consumer spending", and never splits it out. A member who logs in every month is also a player who keeps spending. Since November 2025, GTA Online itself comes with GTA+ on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • Old games, new jobIndirectTen older games, from Bully to L.A. Noire, now work as a reason to stay subscribed, with no new game to build. GTA V's story mode joined the GTA+ games library in June 2026.

Stay or cancel?

A subscription has one job: get paid again next month. Two ways to look at that.

Just the cash

Shark Cards$74.971 × Tiger + 1 × Great White + 1 × Whale: the cheapest cards for the same GTA$

For the cash alone, Shark Cards are $20.91 cheaper. That $20.91 is what the garage, the monthly car and the games cost you.

Try to cancel

Every subscription has a cancel button. What the screen behind it shows you is a product decision.

US store prices. Shark Card amounts are today's, the same as on Day 6. Paying from month one, GTA+ is the cheaper way to get the cash for one or two months; from three months, cards are as cheap or cheaper.

What people complain about

Complaint

A second cash register

At launch, some players called it a cash grab, a GTA community admin told GamesRadar in April 2022, and worried it would grow into content only members could play.

Complaint

Storage behind a paywall

The 100-car garage solved a real problem for collectors, but only for people who pay every month.

Complaint

A price rise nobody announced

The April 2024 rise was spotted by the press. It never appeared on Rockstar's Newswire.

Complaint

Two subscriptions to play online

GTA Online now comes with GTA+ on consoles, but playing online still needs PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass as well, Rockstar's own FAQ says.

Complaint

Last-gen left out

GTA+ has only ever been sold for PS5, Xbox Series X|S and, since 2025, PC. PS4 and Xbox One never got it.

The GTA VI ticket

The only confirmed link between GTA+ and GTA VI is a free month.

With a digital copy of GTA VI

One month of GTA+, free

A digital copy of GTA VI bought before 20 November 2026 comes with one month of GTA+.

Redeem it by 31 March 2027 on PlayStation, or within 180 days on Xbox. Then it renews at the regular monthly price until you cancel. Already a member? Your next bill moves back a month.

Rockstar hasn't said whether GTA+, the garage or the monthly GTA$ will carry into GTA VI. Until it does, this page treats that as an open question.

09

What I'd change

GTA+ is good value if you play every month. The friction is at the edges: a price rise nobody announced, and a free month that turns into a paid one unless you remember to cancel.

As a PM, I'd do three things. Send a reminder a few days before any free month becomes a paid one, and before any price change. Offer a yearly plan to the people who already pay every month. And let non-members buy the big garage once, with GTA$, so people stay for the perks and not because their cars are parked inside.

PM concept · Learn this

Churn and switching costs

Churn is the share of paying members who leave each month. Switching costs are what leaving takes from you: cancel GTA+ and your stored cars stay, but the garage stops taking new ones.

GTA+ fights churn three ways: something new every month, a garage that stops taking cars once you leave, and a library that keeps growing, now with GTA Online itself in it on consoles. The free month with GTA VI works the other end: getting people through the door.

The question for any PM: what keeps someone paying every month for a game they already own? Not the cash. Over a few months, Shark Cards buy the same GTA$ for less. People stay for the things that are annoying to lose.
